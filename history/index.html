Release Notes

Klypp History

A timeline of product and platform milestones.

Digital Releases and My Media browsing refresh

Mobile AppAndroid 2.14 · iOS 2.15
  • Choose a detailed list or a poster grid with one to four posters per row in Digital Releases and My Media, with releases grouped by date.
  • Added view options to include up to 30 days of past releases alongside upcoming titles, with separate saved preferences for each view.
  • Added a movie details view with artwork, digital-release dates, descriptions, and reminder controls.

Snapshot dates and smoother browsing

Mobile AppAndroid 2.14 · iOS 2.15
  • Made Snapshot dates clearer, including digital-release dates for movies and calendar dates for older releases.
  • Improved scrolling, loading, and continuity when switching between Digital Releases and My Media, with more reliable refreshes after reminder changes.
  • Fixed server-update prompts that could be missed while the app was still connecting during startup.

Media lookback and connection recovery

ServerOct 25, 2026
  • Added support for showing up to 30 days of past movies and TV episodes in My Media while keeping upcoming releases included.
  • Improved recovery when the server's authentication certificate is rejected during upload-access checks.
  • Improved account and device registration so repeated mobile sign-ins do not displace the server's certificate.

Android file selection permissions

Android2.14
  • File selection now uses the Android system picker without requesting broad photo, media, or storage permissions.
  • Klypp receives access to the files you select for upload.

iOS startup and incoming files

iOS2.15
  • Improved iOS startup compatibility and handling of incoming files and links when opening or returning to the app.

Password-reset email protection

ServerOct 20, 2026
  • Added rate limits and an account cooldown to reduce repeated password-reset emails.
  • Repeated requests during the cooldown preserve the existing reset link instead of replacing it.

Digital releases and media reminders

Server0.26.1018
  • Added digital-release tracking for movies and support for movie reminders alongside TV reminders.
  • Added reminder syncing, ignore controls, scanner refreshes, and clearer media and reminder status handling.

Android digital releases and Snapshot customization

Android2.9
  • Added Digital Releases and My Media views for upcoming movies, TV episodes, and saved reminders.
  • Added Snapshot customization so users can choose which sections appear and reorder them.
  • Improved reminder indicators, digital-release cards, loading states, and navigation continuity.

Klypp Server dashboard and diagnostics

Server0.26.919
  • Added a dashboard showing Klypp services, relay connection, media-processing status, active transfers, queued files, and deferred files.
  • Added quick links to common server management areas and a Relay diagnostics toggle for enabling verbose connection logging without restarting the server.

Relay connection recovery

Server0.26.919
  • Improved relay health checks so stale or replaced connections are detected and recovered instead of appearing healthy while unable to receive requests.
  • Improved stream handling so connection messages and health checks do not interfere with each other.

Targeted Plex refresh and ready-to-watch notifications

Server0.26.912
  • Klypp now tells Plex exactly which movie, episode, or music album changed instead of relying on Plex's broad library scans or unreliable folder watching, especially with large libraries.
  • After Plex confirms the media is available, Klypp can notify you that it is ready to watch. Music updates are grouped by album to avoid notification spam.
  • Improved relay recovery so missing certificates can be restored automatically without requiring another sign-in.

More reliable media naming and processing

Server0.26.901
  • Copied movie and TV files can now use consistent Plex-style names, including years, season and episode numbers, episode titles where available, and multi-episode ranges.
  • Improved rename-history controls, final filenames in transfer notifications, watched-folder recovery, and support for common media formats.

Rename files during media copy

Server0.26.813
  • Added optional Plex-style naming convention for copied movie and TV files while preserving their extensions.
  • Added rename history with manual rename and undo support so filename changes can be reviewed and restored.

Relay reconnect stability

Server0.26.812
